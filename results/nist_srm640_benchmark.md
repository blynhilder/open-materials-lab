## Limitations

- No experimental measurement of SRM powder was performed.
- NIST Table 1 entries are *information values* (no stated uncertainty), not certified values.
- Only cubic Bragg geometry (peak positions) is checked here; intensities, Debye–Waller,
  absorption, Kα2, and instrument function are out of scope for Increment 2.
- COD and NIST lattice parameters refer to slightly different temperatures; no CTE correction.
